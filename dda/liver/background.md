### Experimental context

Ölander et al. 2020 characterised the proteomes of four major human liver cell types — hepatocytes (HC), liver sinusoidal endothelial cells (EC), Kupffer cells (KC), and hepatic stellate cells (SC) — in order to obtain a cell-type-resolved view of the human liver proteome and to understand how each cell population contributes to hepatic function. Cells were isolated from three individual donors, giving three biological replicates per cell type (12 samples in total). Proteins were quantified by label-free quantitative mass spectrometry on a Q Exactive instrument.

The data are available on PRIDE under accession PXD012615.
